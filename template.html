Backtest assumptions

  • Data source: Yahoo Finance via yfinance, daily adjusted close (split and dividend adjusted). Prices cached locally.
  • Period: 2007-03-01 to most recent fully executed signal (Mode A/B baseline). Backtest start constrained by DBC inception (2006-02-06) plus 12 months of history. Earlier periods held cash. Mode C universes with shorter-history ETFs start later — PDBC universe begins 2015-12.
  • Signal: trailing 12-month rate of change on month-end adjusted close, with a 200-day SMA trend filter (also computed on adjusted close).
  • Mode A — portfolio construction: top two ETFs by ROC, equal-weighted at 50% each. If only one survives the filter, hold it at 50% with 50% in cash. If none survive, 100% cash. The cash-fallback rules are an interpretation of unspecified edge cases, not directly from Teo.
  • Mode B — graduated overlay: same ranking and top-two pick as A, but the risk-asset allocation is sized by a composite score in [0, 100]. Composite is the equal-weight mean of three sub-scores: breadth (count above 200d SMA / 4 × 100); momentum strength (avg ROC of qualifying ETFs, winsorised at [0, 40%], rescaled 0–100); leader trend strength (avg distance of top-two qualifying ETFs above 200d SMA, winsorised at [0, 25%], rescaled 0–100). Allocation map: [0,25)→25%, [25,50)→50%, [50,75)→75%, [75,100]→100%, lower-inclusive. "Qualifying" means above 200d SMA. Zero eligible overrides to 100% cash regardless of composite.
  • Mode C — universe sensitivity: Mode A strategy run on each of five four-ETF universes (baseline plus four single-substitution variants). Bond, equity, and commodity slots are rotated independently so that any sensitivity is attributable to one substitution at a time. Only 0 bps slippage is reported.
  • Rebalance convention: signal computed at prior month-end close; execution at the close of the first trading day of the next month. Rebalanced monthly, including months where the held pair is unchanged.
  • Cash proxy: synthesised from the ^IRX 13-week T-bill yield, daily-compounded. BIL is not used.
  • Slippage variants: 0, 5, and 10 bps round-trip applied to one-way turnover (cost = one_way_turnover × bps / 1e4). The first allocation is taken at zero slippage cost.
  • Risk-free rate for Sharpe and Sortino: dynamic ^IRX, daily compounded.
  • Sortino convention: denominator uses the standard deviation of the subset of negative excess returns. The textbook clipped-zero formulation gives a slightly higher Sortino; the relative ranking versus benchmarks is unchanged.
  • 200-day SMA filter: computed on adjusted close per the user specification. For dividend-paying ETFs (notably TLT), this introduces a small upward bias in the "above SMA" check versus a price-only computation. Material for TLT, sub-30 bps for SPY, zero for GLD.
  • Calendar-year returns: the most recent year is shown as year-to-date. The first partial year (2007) is excluded.
  • Costs not modelled: tax, commissions outside slippage, market impact (immaterial for liquid ETFs at retail size).
Open caveat — single-source price data. Historical adjusted closes are sole-sourced from Yahoo Finance. Stooq's free CSV endpoint now requires an API key, so an automated second-source cross-check is currently not in the pipeline. Inception dates were verified against Wikipedia (GLD, SPY) and the strategy memo (TLT, DBC). A self-consistency check between yfinance's two access paths matched to 0.01 bp on eight randomly selected month-end values across the four ETFs but does not constitute a true second source.
